# Planning a stamina event

The **Stamina** page turns the community stamina spreadsheet into tables for each stamina-based mega event. It covers the events where your progress depends on stamina you spend, not on luck: gacha events, World Tree Cup, Inferno Rally, Sandtopia and Northern Odyssey aren't included.

## Milestones

Each event has a milestone table:

- **Shards**: the UR shards that milestone gives.
- **Total shards**: shards collected by the time you reach it.
- **Stamina** (or points, score or circles, depending on the event): what you need to spend to get there.
- **Items needed**: how many of the event's stamina item (baskets, permits, meals…) that takes after your free stamina runs out.

The second table, **without free stamina**, starts counting after the free stamina from regen, for when you've already used it.

## Gem packs

The gem stamina table shows what the 188, 988 and 1988 gem packs give:

- **1P**: one pack.
- **AO**: every pack in one day.
- **AP**: every pack across the whole event.

The 188 pack is the best value. The biggest packs give the least per gem, so only buy them if you have a lot of gems to spare.

## Ways to get 1 UR

UR fellows from these events need 100 shards. The **Ways** show different plans for getting there across several runs of the event: buying only small packs and spreading shards out, saving everything for one big run, or adding bigger packs to finish in fewer runs.

Each plan lists the shards from each run, the stamina it needs, the packs you buy, the items left over and the gems it costs. The math assumes the worst case: no stamina rebate luck and only just reaching the 6-shard milestone with regen. In practice you'll usually do a bit better, so you can keep some items back.

> Always buy the 188 pack. Skipping packs entirely is possible but slow.

The tables come from a community spreadsheet that is still being updated, so some events are incomplete.
