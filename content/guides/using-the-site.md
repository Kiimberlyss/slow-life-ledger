# Using Slow Life Ledger

## The pages

- **Home**: reminders for maintenance, resets and what's coming next.
- **Stamina**: milestone, pack and plan tables for every stamina event.
- **Guides**: write-ups like this one.
- **Wiki**: every fellow and family member, with their skills and who blesses whom. The data comes from the [fan wiki](https://zik-ascend.github.io/isl-tools/wiki/).
- **My ledger**: your own profile, fellows, family and event timers.

## Your ledger

Your ledger is only visible to you.

- **Profile**: type your in-game name, level, earnings and power. It works out the ratio between earnings and power. Tap the picture to change it.
- **Fellows** and **Family**: add the ones you have, by hand, with Quick edit, by pasting from a spreadsheet, or by importing a screen recording.
- **Events**: timers for the events you're doing, with your goals.
- **Tips**: suggestions worked out from what you've saved.

## Saving

Without signing in, your ledger stays in this browser. Sign in with Google (top right) to save it to your account and open it on any phone or computer.
